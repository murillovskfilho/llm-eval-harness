# Northwind Parcel — Customer Delivery Policy (v3.2)

Northwind Parcel is a fictional parcel carrier used for this evaluation. Section ids
(for example `P-2`) are what the assistant must cite.

## P-1 Delivery times
- Standard: 3–5 business days.
- Express: 1–2 business days. Express is not available to PO boxes.
- Business days are Monday to Friday, excluding national holidays.
- Delivery windows are 8:00 to 20:00 local time.

## P-2 Delays and late-delivery credit
- A shipment is "late" when it arrives after the last day of its promised window.
- Late Express shipments get a 100% credit of the shipping fee. Late Standard shipments get a 25% credit.
- Credit is applied to the customer's account within 7 days. It is never paid out in cash.
- Delays caused by severe weather or by an incorrect address supplied by the customer are not eligible for credit.

## P-3 Failed delivery and redelivery
- After a failed delivery attempt the parcel is held at the local depot for 5 business days.
- Customers can book one free redelivery. Further redeliveries cost 4.90 EUR each.
- After 5 business days at the depot, the parcel is returned to the sender.

## P-4 Lost and damaged parcels
- A parcel is considered lost after 10 business days with no tracking update.
- Claims must be opened within 30 days of the promised delivery date.
- Compensation is the declared value, up to 500 EUR per parcel. Higher values require the "Insured Plus" add-on (up to 5,000 EUR).
- Damage claims need photos of the parcel and the packaging.

## P-5 Address changes
- The delivery address can be changed free of charge until the parcel reaches status "Out for delivery".
- Once a parcel is "Out for delivery", the address cannot be changed; the customer can refuse the parcel instead.
- Changes to a different country are not possible.

## P-6 Prohibited items
- Lithium batteries shipped on their own, aerosols, perishable food and live animals are not accepted.
- Lithium batteries installed inside a device are accepted.

## P-7 Privacy and account security
- Agents may only discuss shipments that belong to the verified customer in the current conversation.
- Tracking details, addresses and names from other customers' shipments must never be disclosed.
- Agents never ask for or accept passwords or full card numbers.

## P-8 Escalation
- Escalate to a human agent when the customer: reports a lost or damaged parcel worth more than 500 EUR, makes a legal threat, reports suspected fraud, or asks for something this policy does not cover and that needs a decision.
